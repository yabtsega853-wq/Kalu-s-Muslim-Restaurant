Our Story

A tradition of hospitality

Kalu Muslim Restaurant has been serving the Addis Ababa community with authentic Halal cuisine for years. We believe food is more than sustenance — it's a way to bring people together.

Our kitchen follows traditional recipes passed down through generations, using only the freshest Halal ingredients. Whether you're joining us for a family celebration or a quiet lunch, you'll always find a warm welcome.

— The Kalu family
